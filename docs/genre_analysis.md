# Member 3: Genre-Based Music Exploration

## 1. Objective
Analyze track representation across genres, evaluate variations in popularity and duration, and compare core audio features (danceability, energy) across major genres.

## 2. Track Representation Across Genres
* Evaluated total track counts per genre to isolate top-represented categories.
* Uneven track distribution can skew cross-genre averages if smaller niche genres contain fewer data points.

## 3. Popularity & Duration Insights
* **Popularity:** Commercial genres (e.g., Pop, Hip-Hop, Dance) display higher mean popularity scores compared to niche acoustic or ambient genres.
* **Duration:** Average song duration varies significantly across genres, with electronic and classical tracks leaning longer, while pop and rap maintain shorter structures (~3.5 minutes).

## 4. Audio Feature Distribution
* **Danceability:** Highly elevated in Hip-Hop, Reggaeton, and Pop genres.
* **Energy:** Driven heavily by Rock, Heavy Metal, and EDM categories.

## 5. Methodological Note on Uneven Track Counts
Comparing overall genre means directly can introduce sampling bias. When genres have unequal sample sizes, high-volume genres produce more reliable statistical averages, whereas low-volume genres are more sensitive to single outlier tracks.

## 6. Conclusion
Genre classification strongly dictates acoustic characteristics, target length, and baseline popularity on streaming platforms.
